Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

Dog Seizure Tracker respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using the Dog Seizure Tracker mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store and protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account and use the App.

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password or password credentials necessary to authenticate your account

We do not use your password for any purpose other than authenticating your account. Passwords should be stored using appropriate security and hashing practices and should not be stored in plain text.

Medication Information

The App allows you to record and manage medication information. This may include:

  • Drug name
  • Drug strength
  • Dose
  • Time or number of hours until the medication is due again
  • Other medication-related information you choose to enter

Medication information may constitute health-related information and is treated as sensitive information.

Pet Information

The App allows you to maintain information about your pet, including:

  • Pet name
  • Breed
  • Age
  • Photographs you choose to upload or take through the App

Seizure Information

The App allows you to record information about seizure events. This may include:

  • Date of occurrence
  • Time of occurrence
  • Duration
  • Symptoms
  • Medication given in response to the event
  • Other information you choose to enter regarding the event

Seizure information is health-related information and is treated as sensitive information.
